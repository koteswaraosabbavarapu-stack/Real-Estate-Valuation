================================================================================
INDIAN METROPOLITAN RESIDENTIAL REAL ESTATE VALUATION DATASET
================================================================================

1. DATASET OVERVIEW:
--------------------
Dataset Name: Housing Prices in Metropolitan Areas of India (6 Major Cities)
Source: Kaggle & Public GitHub Repository
Source URL: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anish2105/House-Price-Prediction/master/
Original Dataset Reference: "Housing Prices in Metropolitan Areas of India" (Kaggle)
License / Usage: Open Data / Academic and Research Use (CC0 / Open Public)
Geographic Coverage: 6 Top Metropolitan Economic Engines in India:
  - Bangalore (Karnataka)
  - Mumbai (Maharashtra)
  - Delhi-NCR (National Capital Region)
  - Chennai (Tamil Nadu)
  - Hyderabad (Telangana)
  - Kolkata (West Bengal)

Total Raw Records: 32,963 property listings
Total Cleaned Records: 27,614 properties after deduplication and outlier boundary validation
Distinct Localities / Neighborhoods: 1,711 verified Indian residential micro-markets

2. TARGET VARIABLE:
-------------------
Target: "Price"
Unit: Indian Rupees (₹ - INR)
Description: Total listed / valuation price of the residential property.
Derived Target Representations:
  - Price_Lakhs: Price in Indian Lakhs (1 Lakh = ₹100,000)
  - Price_per_sqft: Price per square foot (₹/sq.ft)

3. CORE PROPERTY ATTRIBUTES:
----------------------------
1. City (String):
   The metropolitan city in which the property is situated:
   ['Bangalore', 'Mumbai', 'Delhi', 'Chennai', 'Hyderabad', 'Kolkata']

2. Location (String):
   The specific locality, micro-market, or neighborhood (e.g., Whitefield, Indiranagar,
   Bandra West, Andheri East, Gachibowli, Jubilee Hills, Saket, Dwarka, OMR, Salt Lake).

3. Area (Float / Int):
   Total super built-up / living area of the property measured in Square Feet (sq ft).
   Valid residential range: 300 sq ft to 8,500 sq ft.

4. No. of Bedrooms / BHK (Int):
   Number of bedrooms in the housing unit (1 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK, 4 BHK, 5+ BHK).

5. Resale (Int: 0 or 1):
   - 0: New Property / Direct Builder Sale / Under Construction
   - 1: Resale / Pre-owned property

4. AMENITIES AND SPECIFICATIONS (Binary: 0 = Absent/Not Listed, 1 = Present):
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The dataset contains 35 detailed residential amenity flags:
- Essential Utilities: PowerBackup, LiftAvailable, CarParking, Gasconnection, RainWaterHarvesting
- Security & Maintenance: 24X7Security, Intercom, MaintenanceStaff, StaffQuarter
- Lifestyle & Recreation: SwimmingPool, Gymnasium, ClubHouse, SportsFacility, IndoorGames,
  JoggingTrack, LandscapedGardens, Children'splayarea, MultipurposeRoom, GolfCourse, Cafeteria
- Architecture & Wellness: VaastuCompliant
- Neighborhood Infrastructure: ShoppingMall, School, Hospital, ATM
- Furnishings & Appliances: AC, Wifi, BED, TV, DiningTable, Sofa, Wardrobe, Refrigerator, Microwave, WashingMachine

5. FEATURE ENGINEERING SCORES:
------------------------------
- Area_per_BHK: Built-up area divided by number of bedrooms (spatial comfort ratio).
- BHK_x_Area: Non-linear interaction between room count and total floor area.
- Furnishing_Score: Sum of verified home furnishing and appliance items (0 to 9).
- Security_Score: Composite index of security, power, and building maintenance (0 to 5).
- Recreation_Score: Composite index of club, fitness, sports, and green landscape amenities (0 to 9).
- Total_Amenities: Cumulative count of active property amenities (0 to 35).

6. DATASET LIMITATIONS & DISCLAIMER:
------------------------------------
- The dataset reflects residential listing price records across metropolitan hubs.
- Actual negotiated transaction prices may deviate by 5% to 15% from listing prices depending on negotiation, payment terms, and market liquidity.
- Micro-location premiums (such as corner plots, high-floor views, or proximity to metro stations) can introduce unobserved price variance.
- Predictions generated by the system are statistical estimates for educational, analytical, and preliminary valuation purposes, and should not replace formal on-site appraisal by certified RICS/Government-approved evaluators.
